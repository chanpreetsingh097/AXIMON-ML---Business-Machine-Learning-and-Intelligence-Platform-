AXIMON ML – Enterprise Business Machine Learning Platform

AXIMON ML is a unified platform designed to transform business data into intelligent predictions and actionable insights.

Core Capabilities

* 14 integrated ML pipelines for enterprise business challenges.
* Automated data ingestion, validation, cleaning, and feature engineering.
* Model training, hyperparameter tuning, and performance evaluation.
* Explainable AI using SHAP for transparent predictions.
* Customer churn, fraud detection, credit risk, and demand forecasting.
* Customer segmentation, recommendation systems, and sentiment analysis.
* FastAPI-powered prediction APIs and batch inference.
* MLflow-based experiment tracking and model versioning.
* Model monitoring, data drift detection, and automated retraining.
* Interactive business intelligence dashboards and KPI reporting.

Technology Stack

Python | Pandas | NumPy | Scikit-learn | XGBoost | SHAP | FastAPI | MLflow | Docker | PostgreSQL

Vision

To empower organizations with scalable, intelligent, and data-driven business decision-making.
